H&D Co., Ltd. values user privacy and explains in this policy what information may be collected or processed, how it is used, which third-party services may be involved, and what choices users have.

1. Information That May Be Collected or Processed

Multi Ctrl+C.V is a multi-clipboard application that helps users save frequently used text and images and paste them again when needed. The following information may be collected or processed to provide app features, advertising, and subscription features.

1.1 App Functionality Data

  • Text slot data saved directly by the user
  • Image slot data saved directly by the user
  • Image information selected or shared by the user
  • Clipboard-related data managed by the user through app features
  • Slot display, copy, and paste feature usage information

1.2 Photo and Image Information

  • Images directly selected by the user through iOS photo selection features or system share features
  • Image data explicitly selected by the user to be saved in the app
  • Image cache information required to display slots and provide paste-related features

1.3 Advertising and Subscription Information

  • Advertising identifiers, including IDFA where applicable
  • Device information
  • App usage information
  • Ad viewing and ad interaction information
  • Subscription and purchase status information processed through Apple In-App Purchase
  • Information used to verify ad removal or premium feature availability

1.4 App Stability Information

  • Device type and operating system version
  • Error or crash-related information that may occur during app use
  • Non-identifying diagnostic information used to improve app stability

2. Purpose of Use

Multi Ctrl+C.V uses the collected or processed information for the following purposes:

  • Providing text and image slot storage features
  • Allowing users to copy or paste selected text and images again
  • Processing images selected through iOS photo selection features or system share features
  • Displaying saved slot data in the app
  • Providing advertisements and ad removal subscription features
  • Verifying subscription status and processing purchase restoration
  • Improving app stability and analyzing errors
  • Complying with Apple App Store review and related policies

3. Clipboard, Text, and Image Data Processing

Multi Ctrl+C.V may process text and image data that the user directly saves in the app or selects through app features.

Text and image slot data saved by the user may be stored locally on the device to provide app features. H&D Co., Ltd. does not separately upload or store such data on its own external servers.

The app processes text and images explicitly selected or shared by the user. It does not collect or transmit sensitive input information such as passwords, payment details, or private messages that the user did not intend to save in the app.

4. Photo and Image Access

Multi Ctrl+C.V processes images only when the user directly selects them to save into slots or shares them with the app through system share features.

  • The app processes only images directly selected or shared by the user.
  • Image data is used for slot display and paste-related features.
  • Image data may be stored locally on the device or in temporary cache storage.
  • H&D Co., Ltd. does not upload user image data to its own external servers.

5. Third-Party Services

Multi Ctrl+C.V may use the following third-party services to provide app features, advertising, and subscription processing.

5.1 Google AdMob

Multi Ctrl+C.V may use Google AdMob to provide advertisements. During ad delivery, Google AdMob may process advertising identifiers, device information, and app usage information.

For more information, please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

5.2 Apple In-App Purchase

Subscriptions and payments in Multi Ctrl+C.V are processed through Apple In-App Purchase. H&D Co., Ltd. does not collect credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or other direct payment information.

Purchase status information provided by Apple may be used to verify subscription status, provide ad removal features, and process purchase restoration.

6. Advertising Identifier and Tracking Choices

Users can change app tracking permission settings on their iOS device. Depending on the tracking permission setting, personalized advertising may be provided differently.

Users can manage app tracking permission, ad personalization, and related advertising settings through iOS device settings.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Text and image slot data saved by the user may be stored locally on the device to provide app features.

Users may delete saved slot data directly through in-app deletion features. Deleting the app may also delete app data and temporary cache data stored on the device.

Information processed by third-party services for advertising, subscription processing, or purchase verification may be retained or processed according to each service provider’s policy.

8. Disclosure to Third Parties

Except where required by law or with user consent, H&D Co., Ltd. does not sell or provide user personal information to third parties for separate marketing purposes.

However, third-party services such as Google AdMob and Apple In-App Purchase may process information to the extent necessary for advertising, subscriptions, and payment processing.

9. Children’s Privacy

Multi Ctrl+C.V is a multi-clipboard and productivity app intended for general users. H&D Co., Ltd. makes efforts to comply with applicable laws and Apple App Store policies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated due to changes in app features, third-party services, applicable laws, or Apple App Store policies.

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page.
